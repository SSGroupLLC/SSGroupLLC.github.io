About us

A new company built on long careers

2S Group LLC was registered in Georgia in 2026 with one goal: a plant, port or terminal should be able to call one company and get the right expert for any machine on site.

The company is new. The experience behind it is not. Our specialists built their careers maintaining pumps, engines, forklifts, reach stackers, cranes and electrical systems at ports, oil terminals, food and beverage plants and heavy industry.

Instead of one general team, 2S Group works through signed contracts with certified specialists in every discipline: pump technicians, diesel mechanics, diagnostics engineers, electricians, automation engineers and certified welders. Each job gets the depth of a specialist with the simplicity of one supplier.

We combine this field experience with modern maintenance management: planning, risk control, documented work and data that helps you make decisions.

Environment, Health and Safety

Safety is planned before work starts

No job is so urgent that it cannot be done safely. Every task is assessed for risk, and the level of control, supervision and EHS presence is set by the difficulty and hazard of the work.

EHS

EHS meeting before every job

A pre-job meeting (toolbox talk) with the whole crew: scope, hazards, controls and emergency plan.

RA

Risk assessment

A written risk assessment for every risky task, agreed with your site team before work begins.

PTW

Permit to work

Hot work, confined spaces, work at height, electrical and lifting work only under a valid permit.

LOTO

Isolation and lockout

Energy sources are isolated, locked and tagged, and zero energy is verified before work.

PPE

Trained and equipped people

Certified specialists with the right PPE, tools and a site induction for your facility.

STOP

Stop-work authority

Anyone on our team can stop the job if conditions change or something is unsafe.
